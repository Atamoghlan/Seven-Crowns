Frequently Asked Questions

Is my progress saved automatically?
Yes. The game saves automatically, and you can also keep up to three manual saves: Pause → Saves.

Will updating the game delete my progress?
No. Updates keep your saves. Uninstalling the game deletes them, because they are stored only on your device.

Do I need the internet to play?
No. The whole game works offline. The internet is only needed for optional videos and for purchases.

I bought a chapter on another device or reinstalled the game. How do I get it back?
Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Are the videos required?
No. A video only shortens a construction by two days if you choose to watch it. You can play the whole game without them.

Does time pass while the game is closed?
No. Time in the kingdom passes only while you play, and you can pause or speed it up (×2, ×3).

What languages does the game support?
English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil) and Turkish. The game starts in your device language, and you can change it in Settings.

I found a bug or have a suggestion — what should I do?
Email us at dreamgamesllc@gmail.com with as much detail as possible: what happened, what you expected, and your device model. We read every message.
