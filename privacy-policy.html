Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Seven Crowns: Oath of Ashes ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our game on iOS and Android.

Summary: We do not collect any personal data ourselves. The game works offline, needs no account, and keeps your progress on your device. Optional rewarded videos are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device data to show ads. Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. The App does not:

  • Require account creation, sign-in or any personal details (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Send your game progress or settings to us or to any server
  • Access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App offers optional rewarded videos: you choose to watch one to shorten construction in the game. Videos are provided by Google AdMob. To load and show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about ad views and interactions
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information

On iOS, the App asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency; you can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in your device's Settings. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google's consent form is shown first, and you can change your choice later in the game: Settings → Ad privacy. Ads are never required to play.

Google's use of data is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases

Additional story chapters are sold as in-app purchases. Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never receive your payment details. The App only remembers on your device which chapters you own. You can restore purchases in the game: Shop → Restore Purchases.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves your game progress (autosave and save slots), purchased chapters, settings such as language, music and sound, and a small technical log used to diagnose crashes, locally on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us
  • Is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
